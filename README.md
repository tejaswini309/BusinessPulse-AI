# 🚀 BusinessPulse AI – Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

BusinessPulse AI is an enterprise-level business analytics platform designed to transform raw e-commerce operational data into actionable business insights. The project focuses on identifying business performance trends, operational inefficiencies, customer behavior, and revenue opportunities using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

This project is built using the Brazilian Olist E-commerce Dataset and follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data validation and preprocessing to dashboard development and business intelligence reporting.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle with:

- Fragmented business data
- Hidden operational inefficiencies
- Lack of centralized reporting
- Limited visibility into customer purchasing patterns
- Difficulty identifying profit leakage and business opportunities

BusinessPulse AI addresses these challenges by creating a structured analytics platform that supports data-driven decision-making.
