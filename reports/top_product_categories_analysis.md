# Top Product Categories Analysis

## Objective

Identify the top-performing product categories based on the number of products sold to understand customer demand and support inventory planning.

## Observation

The analysis ranks the top 10 product categories by sales volume. These categories represent the products most frequently purchased by customers.

## Business Insights

- High-performing categories contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Popular product categories should receive priority in inventory management and marketing campaigns.
- Lower-performing categories may require promotional strategies or product portfolio evaluation.

## Business Recommendation

- Increase inventory availability for top-selling categories.
- Launch targeted marketing campaigns for high-demand products.
- Monitor category performance regularly using business dashboards.
- Evaluate low-performing categories to improve profitability.
