# Payment Method Analysis

## Objective

Analyze customer payment preferences to understand the most frequently used payment methods and support business decisions related to payment services.

## Observation

The analysis highlights the distribution of payment methods used by customers. It identifies the most preferred payment options and provides insights into customer purchasing behavior.

## Business Insights

- The most frequently used payment method indicates customer preference and should be prioritized for a seamless checkout experience.
- Less frequently used payment methods may require evaluation to determine their business value.
- Understanding payment behavior helps improve customer convenience and optimize payment infrastructure.

## Business Recommendation

- Ensure high availability and reliability of the most popular payment methods.
- Promote secure and convenient digital payment options.
- Monitor payment trends regularly to adapt to changing customer preferences.
- Review low-usage payment methods to optimize operational costs.
