# Monthly Order Trend Analysis

## Objective

Analyze how customer order volume changes over time.

## Observation

The visualization presents monthly order counts across the available dataset period. It helps identify business growth patterns, seasonal demand, and fluctuations in purchasing activity.

## Business Value

- Supports demand forecasting.
- Helps identify seasonal sales periods.
- Assists inventory and resource planning.
- Enables management to monitor business growth over time.
