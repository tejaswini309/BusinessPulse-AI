# Seller Performance Analysis

## Objective

Analyze seller performance based on the number of products sold to identify top-performing sellers and support marketplace optimization.

## Observation

The analysis ranks the top 10 sellers by the total number of products sold. These sellers contribute significantly to marketplace activity and customer demand.

## Business Insights

- Top-performing sellers drive a large share of marketplace transactions.
- High-performing sellers can be rewarded through loyalty or incentive programs.
- Monitoring seller performance helps maintain service quality and operational efficiency.

## Business Recommendation

- Recognize and retain top-performing sellers.
- Provide performance improvement support for low-performing sellers.
- Track seller KPIs regularly through business dashboards.
- Use seller performance metrics for strategic business decisions.

## KPI Tracked

- Products Sold per Seller
- Total Sales per Seller
- Top 10 Sellers
- Seller Contribution
