# Day 7 Executive Summary

## Overview

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was performed on the cleaned Olist e-commerce dataset to understand customer behavior, sales trends, operational performance, and marketplace activity. Multiple visualizations and business reports were created to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Key Business Insights

### Monthly Order Trend
Monthly order analysis revealed purchasing patterns over time, helping identify periods of business growth and seasonal demand.

### Order Status
Most orders were successfully delivered, while canceled and unavailable orders highlight opportunities to improve inventory planning and logistics.

### Payment Methods
Customer payment behavior showed clear preferences for specific payment methods, providing guidance for optimizing payment services and improving the checkout experience.

### Customer Reviews
Review score analysis indicated overall customer satisfaction while highlighting areas where delivery and service quality can be improved.

### Product Categories
The top-selling product categories represent the highest customer demand and should be prioritized for inventory management and marketing campaigns.

### Seller Performance
Top-performing sellers contribute significantly to marketplace activity, making seller performance monitoring an important business KPI.

The insights generated during this analysis can support:
- Strategic business planning
- Inventory optimization
- Customer experience improvement
- Seller performance monitoring
- Executive dashboard development
- Data-driven decision-making
