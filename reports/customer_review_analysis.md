# Customer Review Analysis

## Objective

Analyze customer review scores to evaluate overall customer satisfaction and identify potential areas for service improvement.

## Observation

The review score distribution shows how customers rated their shopping experience on a scale of 1 to 5. This helps identify satisfaction levels and highlights the proportion of positive and negative feedback.

## Business Insights

- Higher review scores indicate positive customer experiences and strong service quality.
- Lower review scores may point to issues related to delivery, product quality, or customer support.
- Monitoring review trends helps businesses improve customer retention and brand reputation.

## Business Recommendation

- Investigate recurring causes of low review scores.
- Improve delivery performance and customer support.
- Collect customer feedback regularly to enhance service quality.
- Track customer satisfaction as a key business KPI.
