# Order Status Analysis

## Objective

Analyze the distribution of customer order statuses to evaluate operational performance.

## Observation

The analysis shows the number of orders in each status, such as delivered, shipped, canceled, processing, invoiced, and unavailable.

## Business Insights

- Delivered orders indicate successful order fulfillment.
- Canceled and unavailable orders may highlight operational or inventory issues.
- Monitoring order status helps improve customer satisfaction and logistics performance.
