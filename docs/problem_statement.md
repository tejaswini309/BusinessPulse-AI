# Problem Statement

## Business Problem

Modern e-commerce companies generate large volumes of operational data from customers, orders, payments, logistics, sellers, products, and reviews. Although this data is available, decision-makers often struggle to identify business issues quickly because information is spread across multiple systems and reports.

BusinessPulse AI aims to consolidate these datasets into a single analytics platform that helps answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should the business do next?

The project focuses on improving business visibility, operational efficiency, and executive decision-making through data analytics and business intelligence.

---

# Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it can:

- Integrate multiple business datasets into one analytics workflow.
- Produce reliable and validated datasets.
- Generate meaningful business KPIs.
- Identify operational trends and performance gaps.
- Provide interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Deliver actionable business recommendations supported by data.
