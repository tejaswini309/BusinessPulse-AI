# Business Questions Answered

During Exploratory Data Analysis, the following business questions were answered:

1. How have monthly orders changed over time?
2. What is the distribution of order statuses?
3. Which payment methods are preferred by customers?
4. How satisfied are customers based on review scores?
5. Which product categories generate the highest sales volume?
6. Who are the top-performing sellers?

These insights form the foundation for SQL analysis, KPI development, and Power BI dashboard creation in the next project phases.
