# 2. Executive Summary

BusinessPulse AI – Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform is an enterprise-level analytics project designed to transform raw business data into actionable insights for decision-makers. The platform focuses on analyzing sales, customer behavior, product performance, logistics, seller performance, payment trends, and operational efficiency using the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset.

Unlike traditional dashboards that only display historical metrics, BusinessPulse AI aims to answer three key business questions:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What should the business do next?

The project integrates SQL, Python, and Power BI to perform data validation, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, business intelligence reporting, and executive dashboard creation. The final outcome is a professional analytics solution that supports strategic decision-making and demonstrates industry-standard data analytics practices.

---

# 3. Business Scenario

Imagine a large e-commerce company that serves millions of customers across different regions. Every day, thousands of orders are placed, shipped, delivered, canceled, and reviewed. The company collects massive amounts of operational data from customers, sellers, logistics partners, payment systems, and product catalogs.

Although this data is available, business teams often struggle to convert it into meaningful decisions. Managers spend significant time collecting reports from different departments instead of focusing on solving business problems.

BusinessPulse AI is designed as an Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform that integrates data from multiple business functions and provides actionable insights. Instead of showing only historical reports, the platform helps decision-makers understand business performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and recommend data-driven actions to improve profitability, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency.
---

# 4. Current Business Challenges

The organization currently faces several business and operational challenges that limit effective decision-making.

- Business data is distributed across multiple systems, making it difficult to obtain a unified view of performance.
- Executives rely on static reports that explain what happened but not why it happened.
- Operational issues such as delivery delays, customer dissatisfaction, payment failures, and order cancellations are difficult to identify early.
- Managers spend significant time preparing reports manually instead of focusing on strategic decisions.
- Hidden business opportunities and operational inefficiencies often remain undiscovered due to limited analytical capabilities.
- Existing reporting systems provide descriptive analytics but lack predictive insights and actionable business recommendations.

These challenges highlight the need for a centralized Decision Intelligence Platform that can transform raw business data into meaningful insights for executive decision-making.
---

# 5. Business Problem Statement

Retail and e-commerce organizations generate massive amounts of operational data from orders, customers, products, payments, logistics, and customer reviews. However, this data is often scattered across different systems, making it difficult for decision-makers to gain a unified view of business performance.

As a result, executives spend significant time identifying business issues, understanding their root causes, and deciding on the best course of action. Traditional reports explain historical performance but often fail to answer why performance changed or what actions should be taken next.

BusinessPulse AI addresses this challenge by providing a centralized Decision Intelligence Platform that transforms operational business data into meaningful insights, interactive dashboards, key performance indicators (KPIs), and evidence-based recommendations to support faster and more informed business decisions.
---

# 6. Business Objectives

The primary objectives of BusinessPulse AI are:

- Build a centralized analytics platform for enterprise decision-making.
- Integrate business data from multiple operational areas into a unified analytical view.
- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs) in real time.
- Analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, logistics, and operational efficiency.
- Identify business trends, patterns, and potential operational issues.
- Support executives with interactive dashboards and data-driven insights.
- Improve business decision-making through evidence-based recommendations.
- Demonstrate industry-standard analytics practices using SQL, Python, and Power BI.
